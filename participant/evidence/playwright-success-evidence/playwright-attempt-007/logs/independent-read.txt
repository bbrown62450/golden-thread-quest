READER 2. The test, the helper and the check

On the marks. It agreed with every NAVIGATION mark and every PRECONDITION mark but one.
  - R4's precondition was half of R4's proof. The proof asserted the typed text £43.20 and not the total the test had read
    from the page. So "equals what they were shown" rested on a precondition, which captures nothing.
  - With the tax left out of the total, R4 as worded holds: 36.00 shown, 36.00 confirmed. The test failed all the same.
On whether each value proves its rule. Each proves a figure for one basket. "PROOF R4 does not prove 'charged'; it proves a
confirmation label." The expected values are typed by hand and never worked out from the figures on the page.
On the helper.
  - A thrown value that counts as false would be recorded as HELD and not thrown again. Playwright throws no such
    thing, so it called this theoretical.
  - If taking the picture failed, that error replaced the assertion's own.
  - "Read" was read a different way from the way the assertion reads, so "exactly" was not exact about white space.
  - The same id used twice would overwrite the first without a word.
On a comment in the test. It said a checkout that printed the discount and ignored it "would pass R1 and fail here", at
R3. The log showed otherwise: with the fault that was tried, it failed at R2 and R3 was never reached.
On the check. Eleven checks claimed more than they tested, or could not fail. The plainest: "for each of the four rules at
least one way of breaking it makes its own assertion fail" compared a table the author had typed with itself. Two checks
on "nothing captured" tested a sentence the helper always prints.
On the limits. The two the log named were honest and not complete. R4's was not named. The 20.01% trick works as well on
the discount, and only the tax was tried. Four faults the application offers were not tried.
On the scan. It listed what the patterns would miss, and said the criterion "holds vacuously: the app has no personal data
or credential to leak."
Its verdict on the seven criteria: six hold with a reservation. The fifth, that breaking the rule changes the evidence,
"holds only for gross breaks".

WHAT WAS CHANGED AFTER READERS 1 AND 2
  The test. R4 now asserts the total the test read from the checkout page, and is worded as what the confirmation says.
    Its precondition is only that a total is there to compare with. The comment was corrected, and the fault that makes
    it true is now run.
  The helper. A flag says whether the assertion failed. The assertion's own error is the one thrown, whatever else goes
    wrong. A passing assertion with no picture fails. An id used twice fails. "Read" is read the way the assertion reads.
  The records. Each now says "for the one basket this test uses" beside its result, and has a line "What this does not
    show".
  The folder. It now holds what Playwright itself reported, and its README is headed for what it is.
  The check. The table check now uses what the runs did. Checks that tested the helper's own sentence were relabelled as
    the helper's word, and the check on Playwright's own output folder carries the criterion. Duplicates were taken out.
    Four more broken applications are run: the total ignoring the discount, a dead button, a wrong subtotal, and a
    discount rate of 10.01%. Three limits are stated as limits. The scan has more patterns and each is shown to fire.
