2. WHAT IT FOUND UNCLEAR OR WRONG

  a. The figure 8.00 was stated as a finding and had never been observed. The test had stopped at line 8, and
     everything after it was read from the application's change.
  b. The diff the report quoted showed the tax worked out through a named two-way switch. That is not something
     written by accident, so "it could be a mistake" was the less likely reading, and the report's "nothing says
     which" was false on its own evidence.
  c. Signs W5 and W6 were "to be tried", and W5 was described as if settled. The deliberate faults were not listed.
  d. "The test has not been touched" was not shown: no checksum value was in the report.
  e. "It passed before, so the test was sound" does not follow from one pass.
  f. "Fails at one line for one reason" is true only because the test stops there. Lines 12 and 15 would fail too.
  g. Lines 13 to 15 never run against the checkout in use until the tax is ruled on.
  h. "Whoever owns the tax rule" names nobody, and in a made-up shop there is nobody.
  i. A red test with no owner gets ignored. Marking it as an expected failure, with a ticket, would keep the
     assertion. The report ruled that out in sign W3 without saying why.
  j. Smaller: the minus sign, the confirmation's currency sign and the unchanged tax rate were asserted and not
     quoted from the diff. The effort figures were invented. Which file to edit, and when, was not said.
