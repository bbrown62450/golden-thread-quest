READER 1, before the real run (85 tests and 54 planted bugs passing at the time)

Its verdict: "I found no path that posts different text, to a different story, site or project, than the preview showed.
The real faults are in what happens after the POST, in the local single-use and expiry controls, and in a few real-API
mismatches that fail safe."
  It traced, and found to hold: a preview and a check cannot write; the text that is sent is the text that was compared;
  the story is named by its id for every read and for the write; a redirect is not followed and credentials are not
  passed on; a change of site fails the fingerprint; the write is sent once and never retried.
  What it found:
  - After a successful write, a read that failed gave "REFUSED ... Nothing has been written", with exit code 1.
  - A failure of the program after the write lost everything it had printed, the comment's identifier included.
  - The saved previews are plain files with no secret behind them. Marking a used one unused, or changing its time,
    would pass. "A forged record cannot post different text, story, site or visibility."
  - Two confirmations of one fingerprint at the same moment could both write.
  - The count after the write went by text and author, and the duplicate check also by visibility.
  - The preview said "on the sandbox allowlist: yes" and did not say which file.
  - Against real Jira: the role's number was sent where its name belongs; a story with no security field was reported
    as having no level; an unrestricted comment on a service project would be seen by customers and nothing said so;
    looking at the story in a browser during a preview would have stopped it; "the story has N comments" means N that
    this identity can see.
  - Secrets: "DB_PASSWORD=...", "client_secret=..." and a web address with a password in it were not refused. More
    characters that cannot be seen were let through.
  - The check printed text read back from Jira as it came, unseen characters and all.
  - Wording: "it was typed or pasted there by a person" is something the program cannot know. The preview did not say
    that the comment goes under the token's account and not under the name given at confirmation.

WHAT WAS CHANGED AFTER READER 1, BEFORE ANYTHING WAS RUN AGAINST REAL JIRA
  Once a write is sent, nothing is reported as not written unless Jira was read and shows it. A failed read or a failed
  program ends with "A PERSON NEEDS TO LOOK" and exit code 2, with what was printed still shown. The comment's
  identifier is saved as soon as Jira gives it. A marker file stops two runs confirming one preview. The count after
  the write uses the duplicate check's own rule. The preview names the allowlist file with its checksum, says under
  whose account the comment would appear, and says it cannot tell whether a project is open to customers. A role is
  sent by name only. A missing security field is "Jira did not say". An unrestricted comment on a service project is
  refused. The snapshot leaves out fields that move when someone only looks. The secret patterns went from eight to
  nine and catch the forms it named. Unseen characters are replaced in what the check prints. The site's address must
  be its name, with no port or path.
  Tests went from 85 to 103. Planted bugs went from 54 to 69. Each fault has a test.
  NOT CHANGED, and written into the rules: the saved previews have no secret behind them.
